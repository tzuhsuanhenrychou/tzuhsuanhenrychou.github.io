Teaching philosophy

My teaching goal is to develop engineers with strong analytical foundations who can reason clearly and make sound design decisions under real-world constraints. My approach emphasizes three principles:

  • Intuition before equations: Begin with physical examples and intuitive predictions before moving to mathematical formulations.
  • Learning by doing: Use short problem sets, design projects, and implementation challenges to deepen understanding.
  • Connecting theory and practice: Explain how idealized models are adapted in deployed communications systems.

Teaching interests

Undergraduate: Signals and Systems; Probability and Random Processes; Introduction to Communication Systems.

Graduate: Digital Communications; Wireless Communications; Detection and Estimation; MIMO Communications and Signal Processing.

Potential new elective: Wireless Standards and System Design: 5G/6G and Wi-Fi, emphasizing synchronization, channel estimation, equalization, coding, and standards-related case studies.

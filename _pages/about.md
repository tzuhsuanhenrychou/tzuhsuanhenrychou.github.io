# Tzu-Hsuan (Henry) Chou, Ph.D.

**Wireless Communications · MIMO Signal Processing · 6G and Next-Generation Wi-Fi**

I am a Wireless Systems Engineer at **Qualcomm Technologies, Inc.**, in San Diego, California. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from **Purdue University** in 2022, where I was co-advised by Professors David J. Love and Nicolo Michelusi. Previously, I worked on 4G LTE software engineering at MediaTek.

My research investigates how to **efficiently acquire high-dimensional wireless channel information and effectively exploit it through MIMO transceiver design**. My work spans massive MIMO, mmWave/sub-terahertz communication, channel and beam training, UAV-enabled relay networks, distributed/cell-free MIMO, and practical transceiver design. At Qualcomm, I contribute to physical-layer technologies for next-generation 3GPP and IEEE 802.11 standards, connecting foundational algorithms with deployable wireless systems.

## Research directions

- **Channel training and acquisition:** Wideband and sub-THz massive MIMO, position-aided beam training, and localization in distributed MIMO systems.
- **MIMO transceiver design:** Distributed beamforming for UAV relays, probabilistic-shaping-aware transceivers, and designs robust to hardware imperfections.
- **Future wireless systems:** Near-field extremely large MIMO (XL-MIMO), low-overhead coordination for distributed/non-terrestrial networks, and decentralized beamforming.
